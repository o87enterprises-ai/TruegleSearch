## 2. Coverage triage — mostly not broken

The page-indexing report listed four "critical issues". Three are Google
reporting correct behaviour:

- **Page with redirect (14)** — the legacy `<Navigate>` routes
  (`/search-portal`, `/results`, `/biased`, `/osint*`, `/search-results`,
  `/shorts`) plus protocol/host variants. None are in the sitemap. Working as
  designed; a reported redirect means Google indexed the destination instead.
- **Blocked by robots.txt (6)** — matched the six `Disallow` rules exactly.
- **Alternate page with proper canonical (3)** — this is a success message.
  Google is confirming it obeyed a canonical we set.
- **Not found, 404 (1)** — `/adframe.html?k=…&d=millionairelucidly…`, a
  leftover **Adsterra** frame from before the 2026-08-24 ad removal. A 404 is
  the correct response for a URL that should no longer exist. `check:ads`
  passes and the only repo matches are historical comments. Nothing to fix;
  the validation already running in GSC will clear it.

**One real bug, fixed this cycle:** `/search` carried both `Disallow: /search`
in robots.txt and `X-Robots-Tag: noindex` in `_headers`. Those cancel — a
blocked path is never fetched, so the noindex is never read, and a linked
blocked URL can still be indexed URL-only. Removed the Disallow, kept the
noindex.

Prerendering was already correct: `prerender.mjs:72-78` deliberately
canonicalises to the trailing-slash form Cloudflare Pages serves, and the
sitemap matches it.

## 3. Two claims on the live site that are no longer true

The skill's own constraint is *"all claims in creative must be true — the
brand IS trust."* Two indexed pages break it:

1. **`/blog/understanding-our-ad-color-system/`** — "What the Ad Colors on
   Truegle Mean — and Why We Show Them" (dated 2026-06-28). Explains a live
   advertising system: 7 mentions of "revenue", 5 of "advertiser", plus
   "sponsored" and "ad slot". Truegle has shown no ads since 2026-08-24.
2. **`/advertise/`** — a media kit selling ad placements ("Native
   In-Results", zone `search-inline`) whose components were deleted. Its own
   source comment says *"Numbers below are placeholders — update AUDIENCE /
   PACKAGES with real figures before sending this to advertisers."*

Both are in the sitemap and indexed. A privacy brand whose top-of-funnel
content explains its ad system, while claiming to serve no ads, is a
credibility problem that no amount of SEO fixes.

**Not actioned — this is a product decision, not a code change.** CLAUDE.md
is explicit that ad monetization decisions are the owner's. Note the policy
bans *third-party ad networks*; a direct-sold, tracking-free sponsorship is
arguably a different thing, so `/advertise` may be intentional. Needs a
ruling either way:

- If direct sponsorship is still on the table → update `/advertise` with real
  figures and rewrite the blog post to describe the current reality.
- If not → unpublish both, remove from sitemap, let them 404 or redirect.

## 4. Competitive read

Privacy search is **4–6% of global queries**, split DuckDuckGo ~65%, Brave
~18%, Startpage ~12%, Ecosia ~5%.

| Competitor | Their moat |
|---|---|
| DuckDuckGo | Distribution and brand. Ranking described as stagnant but "good enough" |
| Brave | Independent index built from scratch — the strongest technical claim in the space |
| Kagi | Paid, ad-free, result-type filters, de-ranked promoted content |
| Startpage | Google-quality results, EU privacy framing, Anonymous View proxy |

**The strategic problem, stated plainly:** our blog is targeting
*"search without tracking"*, *"alternative to Google"*, *"how to search
privately"*, *"is DuckDuckGo really private"*. Those are the highest-authority
terms in a category where four incumbents have years of backlinks and
DuckDuckGo owns two thirds of the market. A 14-page site with no backlink
profile cannot rank for them. That is why impressions are ~1/day — not a
technical fault.

**Where nobody is standing:** none of the four competes on
*multi-perspective* or *anti-censorship*. Brave sells an index, Kagi sells
quality, Startpage sells EU privacy, DDG sells the brand. Perspective
isolation — Wonderland, the blue/red/purple/ocean modes — is genuinely
unoccupied ground.

Tellingly, our two best-positioned existing posts are the two *least* about
privacy: "What Is a Filter Bubble — and How to Escape It" and "Why Seeing
Multiple Perspectives Makes You Better Informed".

## 5. The three plays for this cycle

The skill says at most three. These are chosen to stop competing where we
lose.

### Play 1 — Move the target phrases off "privacy" and onto "perspective"

Stop optimising for `unbiased search engine` / `search without tracking` as
primary. Keep them as secondary. Lead instead with terms that have real
intent and no incumbent:

- "see both sides of a story search"
- "search engine that shows multiple perspectives"
- "how to escape a filter bubble"
- "uncensored search engine"
- "compare perspectives on a news story"

These are lower volume. That is the point: 1% of a term nobody owns beats 0%
of a term DuckDuckGo owns. This is a rewrite of titles and opening 15 words
on the two existing perspective posts, plus the phrase list the next posts
are written against — not new infrastructure.

### Play 2 — Resolve the two false claims

See §3. It is a one-decision, one-afternoon fix, and until it is done every
other trust-based marketing message is undercut by our own indexed pages.

### Play 3 — Directory and listing placements

The skill's "sittings", and the only backlink source available at $0 that
isn't spam: AlternativeTo, PrivacyGuides / PrivacyTools forums, Product Hunt,
GitHub awesome-lists (awesome-privacy, awesome-search-engines). This is the
one play that addresses the actual root cause — no backlink profile — rather
than the symptoms.

**Requires the owner to post from their own accounts**, so it should become a
`user-task-instructions` walkthrough rather than an agent task.

## 7. What was deliberately NOT done, and why

**No revenue projections.** The skill's phase 5 models Adsterra CPMs and
Impact.com reapplication at 50K/mo. Ads were removed 2026-08-24 and CLAUDE.md
states the project is no longer run on a profit/loss basis. Producing
"$47/mo at 5K daily views" from a dead ad model would be inventing a number,
and the financial skill exists to stop exactly that. If direct sponsorship is
revived (§3) the model can be rebuilt from real rates.

**No usage statistics section.** Phase 3 wants Cloudflare Analytics and
`/api/search/trending`. Neither is reachable from the build sandbox, and the
backend was mid-outage during this cycle. The GSC figures above are the only
measured numbers in this document; everything else is labelled as reasoning.

**No creative production.** Phase 6 (image prompts, infographics, 30-day
calendar) is premature while §3 is unresolved — creative built on "we don't
show ads" while two indexed pages explain our ad system would have to be
thrown away.
